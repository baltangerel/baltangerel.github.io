About me

I'm an aerospace engineer with an MEng in Aerospace Engineering from the University of Sheffield. I enjoy the full design cycle: sketching an idea, modelling it in CAD, analysing it, and then making and testing a real prototype.

Alongside engineering I build software and machine learning models, mostly in Python: cleaning data, comparing models fairly and turning results into clear decisions. I like projects where the two overlap.

Outside of work I follow the space and aviation industries closely, from new launch vehicles to sustainable aircraft, and I enjoy flight simulation and building small engineering side projects to try out ideas. To stay active I go to the gym regularly, run, and go hiking and climbing, which I find a good way to switch off and stay disciplined.
